# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 18, 2026**

Outfits ("the app") is a local-first wardrobe and outfit-planning app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**
There is no account, no backend server, and no network connection of any kind between the app and its developer (or anyone else). Everything you add to the app — clothing photos, folders, collections, saved outfits, settings — stays only on your device, for as long as you keep it there.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Clothing photos** you take or pick are copied into the app's own private storage area on your device (its sandboxed documents directory) and never leave it.
- **Wardrobe data** — categories, folders, collections, saved outfits, and settings like language or which categories you've hidden — is kept in a single local file, also inside that private storage area.

Deleting the app deletes this data with it. There is no cloud backup, no
sync, and no copy of any of it anywhere else — including with the developer.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera** — only used when you choose to take a photo of a clothing item to add to your wardrobe. The photo is saved locally, as above; it isn't uploaded anywhere.
- **Photo library** — only used when you choose to pick an existing photo (or several) of clothing to add. Same as above: copied into local storage, nothing is uploaded.

The app doesn't access either unless you actively choose to take or pick a photo.

## Third-party services

The app doesn't include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting
SDKs, and doesn't communicate with any third-party service on your behalf.

The "About" screen has two optional links — to the app's
[source code on GitHub](https://github.com/vaclavparma/outfits-app) and to a [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/vaclavparma) page — which open in your device's browser only if you tap them. The app itself sends nothing to either site; whatever you do on those pages is covered by GitHub's and Buy Me a Coffee's own privacy policies, not this one.

## Children's privacy

The app doesn't collect any data from anyone, including children, so there's nothing to say beyond the rest of this policy: nothing is collected, from any user, of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve network access — this file will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
