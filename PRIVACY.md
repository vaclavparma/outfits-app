# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 21, 2026**

Outfits ("the app") is a local-first wardrobe and outfit-planning app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

**The app never sends your data anywhere: not to its developer, not to anyone else.**
There is no account, no backend server, and no network connection of any kind between the app and its developer (or anyone else). Everything you add to the app — clothing photos, wardrobes, outfits, your diary, settings — and the stats calculated from it stay only on your device, for as long as you keep them there.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Clothing photos** you take or pick are copied into the app's own private storage on your device (its sandboxed documents directory) and never leave it, unless you export them yourself.
- **Wardrobe data** — your wardrobes, kinds and types of clothing, clothing details (name, seller, size, price, note), saved outfits, the diary, and settings such as language or the reminder time — is kept in a single local file in the same private storage.
- **Colors** of your clothing are detected on your device.
- **Stats** — how often you wear each piece, cost per wear, the colors you own and wear — are calculated on your device from your diary and wardrobe, and aren't sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes this data with it. The app keeps no copy of it anywhere else — including with the developer.

## Backups

- **Export** — in Settings, you can export everything to a single .zip file. It's created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it. Importing a backup replaces the data in the app.
- **System backups** — depending on your device settings, the app's data may be included in your iCloud or Google backup. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera** — only used when you choose to take a photo of a clothing item. The photo is saved locally, as above; it isn't uploaded anywhere.
- **Photo library** — only used when you choose to pick existing photos of clothing. Same as above: copied into local storage, nothing is uploaded.
- **Notifications** — only used if you turn on the daily diary reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere for it.

The app doesn't access any of these unless you actively choose to use them.

## Third-party services

The app doesn't include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and doesn't communicate with any third-party service on your behalf.

- Now and then, the app may show the system's rating prompt, which is provided by Apple or Google. "Rate Outfits" in Settings opens the app's page in the App Store or Google Play.
- Settings has two optional links — to the app's [source code on GitHub](https://github.com/vaclavparma/outfits-app) and to a [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/vaclavparma) page — which open in your browser only if you tap them. The app itself sends nothing to either site; whatever you do there is covered by GitHub's and Buy Me a Coffee's own privacy policies.
- The app is distributed through the App Store and Google Play, which may collect information about downloads under their own privacy policies.

## Children's privacy

The app doesn't send data about anyone anywhere, including children: it sends nothing, for any user, of any age.

## Deleting your data

You can delete clothing, outfits, diary entries or whole wardrobes in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve network access — this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
